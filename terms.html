Terms of use

These terms cover the website footyhub.tv and the FootyHub TV broadcasts on YouTube and Twitch. By using the site or watching a broadcast you accept them. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the site.

1Who we are

FootyHub TV is an independent channel, run by a small independent team of football fans and engineers. We are not a network, not a betting company and not a rights-holder. You can reach us at contact@footyhub.tv.

2The hosts are AI

LIA, our host and commentator, and Victor Profét, our analyst and tipster, are original AI characters. They are not real broadcasters and they are not based on real people. Their voices are AI, generated with ElevenLabs, and their pictures are AI-generated too.

The hosts' commentary and analysis are written by AI. Much of it is generated live during the match; some of it is prepared before the broadcast or comes from short ready-made phrases, and parts of the show, such as the countdown, the anthem and the adverts, are recorded in advance. Because it is AI, it can be wrong: a name, a number, a call. Do not treat what the hosts say as a record of the match.

3The football is real

The match is real live football, with real data from public feeds. We do not simulate, script or alter anything that happens on the pitch. We do not show footage of the match: what you see on screen are our own graphics.

Live scores, line-ups and match events come from public live football data feeds. Figures like xG come from those same feeds, and win probabilities are an illustrative output of our own model. They are there to add colour and are not official statistics. Data can arrive late, incomplete or wrong, and we cannot promise otherwise.

Victor's picks, his Triple Bet, and any odds, probabilities or predictions you hear or see are opinions, made for entertainment. They are not financial advice and not betting advice, and they are no promise of any result. What happened before says nothing certain about what happens next.

We are not a bookmaker. We do not take bets and we do not handle anyone's money. If you choose to bet, you do it with a licensed operator, where it is legal for you, and entirely at your own risk. Never bet more than you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, stop, and look for a support service in your country.

Privacy policy

This site asks you for nothing: no account, no sign-up form, no advertising. This is everything that happens with personal data, here and in our broadcasts.

1Who we are

FootyHub TV ("we") runs the website at footyhub.tv and the FootyHub TV broadcast channel. For any privacy question, contact us at contact@footyhub.tv.

2When you visit the site

  • Delivering the page. The site is served by GitHub Pages, and one small file with our live status comes from our storage at Cloudflare. Like every web server, they receive your IP address and basic details of your browser, to deliver the page and keep it secure. We do not use this to identify anyone.
  • Counting visits. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: which page was opened, which site led to it, the country, and the type of device and browser. It works without cookies, it does not follow you across other sites, and we only ever see totals, never who you are.
  • Nothing else. Apart from that visit counter, fonts, pictures and scripts come from our own site. YouTube, Twitch and Instagram are plain links: they receive nothing from you until you open them. We do not run third-party advertising or tracking pixels.

3Live chat during a broadcast

If you write in the live chat on YouTube or Twitch during a broadcast, our system reads your public name and your message so that the hosts can react. They can be shown on screen, read out, and passed to the AI services that write and voice the hosts' replies (OpenAI and ElevenLabs). They stay in the recording of the broadcast and in our broadcast logs.

YouTube and Twitch run the chat and hold your account under their own privacy policies. We never receive your email address or any private detail of your account.

4If you write to us

If you email us, we keep your address and your message for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep track of the conversation.

5Details from the earlier version of this site

Until November 2026 this site also offered match alerts, a free prediction game (the Predictor) and a sponsor enquiry form. They are not on the site now. If you used them, we may still hold what you gave us then:

  • Match alerts: the email address you gave us and/or a web-push subscription from your browser.
  • Predictor sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture from Google, plus the username and score predictions you created.
  • Sponsor enquiries: the name, company, email, budget and message you submitted.

We use alert sign-ups only to send the match alerts you asked for, and the rest only for what it was given for. You can stop the alerts or have any of this deleted at any time: write to contact@footyhub.tv.

6Why we may use it

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example the GDPR), it is your consent for the alerts and the Predictor sign-in, and our legitimate interest in everything else: running and protecting the site, counting visits without identifying anyone, running the live show, and answering the people who write to us.

7Who processes it

GitHub hosts the site. Cloudflare stores our files, counts the visits and runs the alerts and the Predictor data. Google provided the sign-in of the Predictor. During a broadcast, OpenAI and ElevenLabs write and voice the hosts' replies to the chat. These providers process data on our behalf; we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Some of them are based outside the European Union, mainly in the United States, and handle data under their own data-protection terms.

8How long we keep it

Visit counts are totals from the start and never point to a person. Emails are kept while we still need them. Chat messages stay in the recording for as long as the video is online, and in our logs for as long as they help us run and improve the show. Details from the earlier version of the site are kept until they are no longer needed for what they were given for, or until you ask us to delete them.

9Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use or to withdraw your consent, by emailing contact@footyhub.tv. You can turn off push alerts in your browser or system settings. If you believe we have handled your data wrongly, you can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

10Under 18

The site and the broadcasts are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11Changes

We may update this policy; important changes will be posted on this page.

Cookies

This site sets one cookie, called fh_cookies. It is stored when you press "Got it" on the cookie notice, it remembers only that you did, it says nothing about you, and it lasts one year.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies, and counting visits works without cookies.

If you visited the site before November 2026, your browser may also hold what the earlier version stored: a random identifier and your Predictor choices in local storage, and a small offline copy of the site that also delivers match alerts if you asked for them. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
