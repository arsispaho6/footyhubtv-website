FootyHub Predictor — Rules & Terms

1. The contest

The FootyHub TV Predictor is a free-to-enter, skill-based prediction game running for the duration of Mundial 2026 (11 June – 19 July 2026). Players predict match scores; points are awarded for accuracy and the top players win prizes. No purchase is necessary and no payment is required to enter or to win.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 18 or over at the time of entry. Void where prohibited by law. By entering you confirm you meet these requirements. Employees of FootyHub TV and their immediate families are not eligible to win prizes.

3. How to enter

Sign in with a valid Google account, choose a username, and submit a score prediction for a match before that match kicks off. You may submit one prediction per match and may change it any time up until kickoff. Predictions cannot be made or changed after kickoff. You can only predict matches scheduled for the current match day. Subscribing to FootyHub TV on YouTube is encouraged but is not required to enter or to win.

4. Scoring

Correct result (win / draw / loss) = 1 point. Exact correct score = 3 points. Points accumulate across every match of the tournament.

5. Leaderboard & ties

Players are ranked by total points. Ties are broken by: (a) most exact scores, then (b) most matches predicted, then (c) earliest to join. In the unlikely event of a remaining tie for a prize position, FootyHub TV may use a tie-break question or split the prize at its discretion.

6. Prizes

1st place: 1 year of Victor Premium. 2nd place: 6 months of Victor Premium. 3rd place: 3 months of Victor Premium. The top three also receive a live shoutout from LIA & Victor during the Final broadcast, plus surprise prizes announced during the tournament. Prizes are personal, non-transferable and have no cash alternative.

7. Winner selection & notification

Winners are the top-ranked eligible players on the final leaderboard once all tournament matches are settled. Winners are contacted by email at the address linked to their account within 7 days of the Final. If a winner cannot be reached or does not respond within 14 days, FootyHub TV may award the prize to the next eligible player.

8. Fair play

One account per person. Creating multiple accounts, impersonating others, using bots or automation, or otherwise manipulating the contest is prohibited and will result in disqualification and removal from the leaderboard. FootyHub TV's decisions on fair play are final.

9. Not gambling

This is a free, skill-based prediction game for entertainment only. It is not betting or gambling, involves no stake or wager, and is not financial advice.

10. Your data

To run the contest we collect your email, name and profile picture from your Google sign-in, plus your chosen username and predictions. We use these to operate the contest, rank the leaderboard, contact winners and send match-related notifications. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion of your account data by emailing contact@footyhub.tv.

11. Liability

FootyHub TV is not responsible for lost, delayed or misdirected entries, technical failures, or circumstances beyond its reasonable control. To the extent permitted by law, FootyHub TV's liability is limited to the value of the prize.

12. Changes

FootyHub TV may modify, suspend or cancel the contest or these terms at any time, including for legal reasons or events beyond its control. Material changes will be posted on this page.

13. Contact

The contest is run by FootyHub TV. Questions: contact@footyhub.tv.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, YouTube or Google.
