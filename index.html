Frequently asked

Everything you want to know

New here? Here's the honest rundown — what FootyHub TV is, what's real, what's AI, and how to watch. Tap a question to open it.

What is FootyHub TV?▾
FootyHub TV is an AI-driven live football channel on YouTube. We broadcast every match of World Cup 2026 (the Mundial) — 104 matches, 48 nations, June 11 to July 19 — with our own AI commentary team calling the action live. Free to watch, with a free Predictor game alongside every match.
Is the football real?▾
Yes — 100%. The match is real live football with real data from public feeds. We don't simulate, script or alter anything that happens on the pitch. What's AI is the commentary: LIA and Victor call the real game as it unfolds.
Are LIA & Victor real people?▾
No. LIA (our host & commentator) and Victor (our analyst & tipster) are original AI characters — not real broadcasters and not based on real people. Their voices are AI, generated with ElevenLabs. Each match also features two AI fans, one for each nation playing, in the stands.
Is it free to watch?▾
Yes. Every broadcast is free on our YouTube channel, and the Predictor game is free to play too. Subscribe on YouTube and we'll let you know the moment we go live.
Is this gambling?▾
No. FootyHub TV is entertainment, and the Predictor is a free, skill-based game — you predict match scores, no money is staked and nothing is bought. Prizes are Victor Premium and surprise prizes, not cash. It's intended for an 18+ audience.
How does the AI actually work?▾
As the real match plays out, our system reads live data from public feeds and turns it into commentary in real time. LIA narrates the action, Victor adds analysis and tips, and the two AI fans react like supporters in the crowd. The voices you hear are AI-generated with ElevenLabs.
Where do the scores & stats come from?▾
Live scores, line-ups and match events come from public live football data feeds and the official group standings. Figures like xG and win probability are illustrative outputs of our own models — they're there to add colour, and are not official statistics.
How do I watch?▾
On our YouTube channel, @FootyHub_TV — also restreamed on Twitch. The easiest way is to subscribe on YouTube and turn on notifications, so it tells you the second we kick off. You can also check the full schedule here on the site.
What's the Predictor & what can I win?▾
The Predictor is our free game: call the score of each Mundial match before kickoff to earn points (right winner = 1 pt, exact score = 3 pts) and climb the season leaderboard. The top predictors win Victor Premium plus surprise prizes, and the top three get a live shoutout from LIA & Victor during the Final. Play the Predictor →
What languages is it in?▾
English, for now. The entire broadcast — LIA, Victor and the fans — is in English throughout the Mundial. More languages may follow down the line.
Who's behind FootyHub TV?▾
A small independent team of football fans and engineers building the channel we always wanted to watch. Got a question, a sponsorship idea or feedback? Reach us at contact@footyhub.tv or via our sponsors page.
Still curious? Email us or subscribe on YouTube so you never miss a kickoff.

FootyHub Predictor — Rules & Terms

1. The contest

The FootyHub TV Predictor is a free-to-enter, skill-based prediction game running for the duration of Mundial 2026 (11 June – 19 July 2026). Players predict match scores; points are awarded for accuracy and the top players win prizes. No purchase is necessary and no payment is required to enter or to win.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 18 or over at the time of entry. Void where prohibited by law. By entering you confirm you meet these requirements. Employees of FootyHub TV and their immediate families are not eligible to win prizes.

3. How to enter

Sign in with a valid Google account, choose a username, and submit a score prediction for a match before that match kicks off. You may submit one prediction per match and may change it any time up until kickoff. Predictions cannot be made or changed after kickoff. You can only predict matches scheduled for the current match day. Subscribing to FootyHub TV on YouTube is encouraged but is not required to enter or to win.

4. Scoring

Correct result (win / draw / loss) = 1 point. Exact correct score = 3 points. Points accumulate across every match of the tournament.

5. Leaderboard & ties

Players are ranked by total points. Ties are broken by: (a) most exact scores, then (b) most matches predicted, then (c) earliest to join. In the unlikely event of a remaining tie for a prize position, FootyHub TV may use a tie-break question or split the prize at its discretion.

6. Prizes

1st place: 1 year of Victor Premium. 2nd place: 6 months of Victor Premium. 3rd place: 3 months of Victor Premium. Victor Premium is scheduled to launch in 2026; winners' subscriptions are activated when it goes live. The top three also receive a live shoutout from LIA & Victor during the Final broadcast, plus surprise prizes announced during the tournament. Prizes are personal, non-transferable and have no cash alternative.

7. Winner selection & notification

Winners are the top-ranked eligible players on the final leaderboard once all tournament matches are settled. Winners are contacted by email at the address linked to their account within 7 days of the Final. If a winner cannot be reached or does not respond within 14 days, FootyHub TV may award the prize to the next eligible player.

8. Fair play

One account per person. Creating multiple accounts, impersonating others, using bots or automation, or otherwise manipulating the contest is prohibited and will result in disqualification and removal from the leaderboard. FootyHub TV's decisions on fair play are final.

9. Not gambling

This is a free, skill-based prediction game for entertainment only. It is not betting or gambling, involves no stake or wager, and is not financial advice.

10. Your data

To run the contest we collect your email, name and profile picture from your Google sign-in, plus your chosen username and predictions. We use these to operate the contest, rank the leaderboard, contact winners and send match-related notifications. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion of your account data by emailing contact@footyhub.tv.

11. Liability

FootyHub TV is not responsible for lost, delayed or misdirected entries, technical failures, or circumstances beyond its reasonable control. To the extent permitted by law, FootyHub TV's liability is limited to the value of the prize.

12. Changes

FootyHub TV may modify, suspend or cancel the contest or these terms at any time, including for legal reasons or events beyond its control. Material changes will be posted on this page.

13. Contact

The contest is run by FootyHub TV. Questions: contact@footyhub.tv.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, YouTube or Google.

FootyHub TV — Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

FootyHub TV ("we") runs the website at footyhub.tv and the FootyHub TV broadcast channel. For any privacy question, contact us at contact@footyhub.tv.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the site:

• Predictor sign-in — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture, plus the username and score predictions you create.
• Match alerts — if you ask to be notified, we store the email address you give us and/or a web-push subscription from your browser.
• Sponsor enquiries — if you use the sponsor form, we store the name, company, email, budget and message you submit.
• Anonymous IDs — we keep a random identifier in your browser's local storage to remember your poll votes and predictions. This is not linked to your identity unless you sign in.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we do not run third-party advertising or tracking pixels.

3. Why we use it (legal basis)

We use your data to operate the Predictor and leaderboard, contact winners, send the match alerts you asked for, reply to sponsor enquiries, and keep the site working. Where the law requires a legal basis (e.g. GDPR), it is your consent (sign-in, alerts) and our legitimate interest in running the service.

4. Who processes it

Sign-in is provided by Google. Form submissions, predictions and alerts are handled by our infrastructure on Cloudflare (Workers / R2). These providers process data on our behalf; we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

Predictor and alert data is kept for the duration of the tournament and a reasonable period after, then deleted. Sponsor enquiries are kept while we are in contact. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing contact@footyhub.tv. You can clear the anonymous IDs yourself by clearing your browser storage, and turn off push alerts in your browser/OS settings.

7. Cookies & storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage only for the functional IDs described above and for your sign-in session.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page.
